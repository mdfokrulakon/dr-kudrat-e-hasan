Know the signs

Warning signs that need urgent help

A heart attack can feel different from person to person. Do not wait to see if symptoms pass.

🫀

Chest discomfort

Pain, pressure, tightness or heaviness in the centre of the chest.

💫

Pain that spreads

Discomfort moving to the arm, shoulder, jaw, neck or back.

💨

Shortness of breath

Difficulty breathing, with or without chest discomfort.

💧

Cold sweat or nausea

Sweating, sickness or light-headedness together with other symptoms.

⚠️

Fainting

Collapse or fainting with chest symptoms, or sudden severe weakness.

If you or someone near you has these symptoms:Call 999 now

Everyday heart care

Habits that protect your heart

General guidance for adults from the World Health Organization. Your doctor will tailor advice to you.

Minutes a week

At least 150 minutes of moderate activity, such as brisk walking.

Salt a day

Less than 5 g of salt (about one teaspoon) in total each day.

Fruit & vegetables

At least 400 g (five portions) of fruit and vegetables daily.

Tobacco

No form of tobacco is safe for the heart. Support to quit is available.

Understanding a blood pressure reading

A reading such as 120 / 80 has two numbers. The top number (systolic) is the pressure as the heart beats; the bottom (diastolic) is the pressure between beats. Adults whose repeated readings are 140/90 mmHg or higher are generally considered to have high blood pressure, which often causes no symptoms, so regular checks matter. Your doctor will advise your own targets.
